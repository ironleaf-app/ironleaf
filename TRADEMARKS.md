## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your software works with Ironleaf, connects to Ironleaf, or opens Ironleaf vaults
  ("works with Ironleaf", "an MCP client tested with Ironleaf", "a plugin for Ironleaf").
- Refer to Ironleaf by name in articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and package listings of the unmodified app.
- Build Ironleaf from source for your own use, and distribute unmodified source.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** build (a fork), you must:

- use a different name that is not confusingly similar to "Ironleaf", and a different icon and logo;
- change the app id (`com.kaitiaki.neuralvault`), the update site (`UPDATE_ORIGIN`), the update and licence public
  keys, and the `ironleaf://` link scheme, so your users do not receive our updates or appear to be ours;
- not suggest that Kaitiaki IT made, endorses or supports your build.

"Based on Ironleaf" or "a fork of Ironleaf" in your description is fine, as long as your product name is your own.
