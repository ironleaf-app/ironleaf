# Welcome to Ironleaf

Ironleaf keeps your notes as plain Markdown files in a folder on your PC. This sample vault is one of those folders: open any note in another text editor and it reads the same.

## Try these

- Ctrl/Cmd+click a link such as [[Linking notes]] to follow it.
- Press **Ctrl+O** to jump to any note, or **Ctrl+K** for every command.
- Open the graph with **Ctrl+G** to see how [[Linking notes]], [[Daily notes]] and [[Chat with your vault]] connect.
- Open the **Auto-link** tab on the right. It spots text like Weekly review that matches another note's title.

Type `[[` anywhere to link a note. Links to notes that don't exist yet, like [[Ideas inbox]], are created when you open them.

## From outside Ironleaf

- Links that start with `ironleaf://` open a note, search or add to today's note from another app.
- Other AI apps on this PC can use your notes through the `ironleaf mcp` command (Settings › Agents).

Everything you write stays in files like this one.
