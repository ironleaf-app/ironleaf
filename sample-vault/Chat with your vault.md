# Chat with your vault

The **Chat** tab answers questions from your notes. Ironleaf searches the vault for the most relevant sections, sends only those excerpts to Claude, and Claude cites the notes it used as links you can click.

It needs a Claude API key (Settings) or the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` environment variable.

Try asking:

- "How do links and backlinks work?"
- "What's in my weekly review routine?"

The Auto-link tab can also ask Claude to suggest links between notes, such as connecting this note to Linking notes.
